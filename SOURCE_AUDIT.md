# Reference and originality audit

## Reference used

The redesign was evaluated against the user-supplied Garden Galaxy screenshots and its
public Steam description. Only broad genre and presentation principles were used:

- a floating low-poly tile diorama in generous negative space;
- randomized set-based decorating rewards;
- visitor-delivered coins and idle arrivals;
- storage, selling, collections, loot-table control, and environment customization.

## Original implementation

No reference-game files, models, textures, shaders, audio, source code, UI layouts, icons,
characters, names, or branding are present in this repository.

The following are original to this project:

- the Bloomforge flower-kiln silhouette and animations;
- all three Mote visitor variants;
- every procedural ground and prop mesh in `scripts/visual_factory.gd`;
- the Meadow/Hearth/Tide sets and their palettes;
- the Botanist's Beacon and Carpenter's Hushbell mechanics;
- the cream-card interface and all generated sound effects;
- all gameplay, persistence, placement, reward, and visitor code.

The original Imota repository at `/Users/luka/Documents/dev/imota-idle` was not modified.
